# Limitations and responsible use

- The included inputs are simulated and cannot establish real-world predictive effectiveness.
- Scores represent structured judgments. They can embed measurement error, missing context, or assessor bias.
- AHP weights reflect stakeholder preferences. The consistency ratio checks internal coherence, not ethical correctness or factual validity.
- TOPSIS depends on normalization, criterion directions, weights, and the alternatives currently included. Adding or removing applications can change relative positions.
- Rank order does not measure monetary value, causal impact, feasibility, legal eligibility, or value for money.
- Requested amount appears beside results but does not influence the primary impact ranking. A funding allocation model needs explicit budget, eligibility, equity, and objective assumptions.
- The tool needs governance, independent review, appeals, privacy protection, and bias assessment before any consequential use.

Possible future work includes panel aggregation, uncertainty intervals, out-of-sample outcome monitoring, fairness constraints, and a clearly specified budget-constrained portfolio model.
